# Custamo Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 23, 2026*

Custamo is a personal macOS app for daily organizing. It is built and used by one person, its developer, and is not offered to the public.

## What Custamo accesses

- **Gmail, read-only** (`gmail.readonly`). Custamo reads email to show unread mail, summarize it, and turn important messages into tasks. It cannot send, delete or change email.

## Where the data goes

- Email data stays **on the user's own Mac**. Custamo has no server and no database outside that computer.
- To write summaries, the text of an email may be sent to **Anthropic's Claude** AI service under the user's own account. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the user's Mac.

## What Custamo never does

- It never sells, shares, or rents data.
- It never uses data for advertising.
- It never gives data to anyone other than the AI service named above.

## Google API Services

Custamo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the app from the Mac deletes all of its data.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
